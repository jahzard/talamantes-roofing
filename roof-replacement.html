Roof replacement

Know what your roof needs before you replace it.

Repeated leaks, widespread shingle damage, or a roof that needs frequent attention are reasons to arrange an inspection. The right next step depends on the condition of the roof, not just how it looks from the street. We can discuss a repair alongside a full replacement so you can make an informed choice.

Common questions

Do I need a replacement or a repair?

A localized problem may be repairable. Broader deterioration or recurring issues may make replacement worth considering. A roof inspection helps clarify the options for your property.

How long will a roof replacement take?

Timing varies with the size and complexity of the roof, material availability, weather, and the condition underneath the old roofing. Ask for a project-specific schedule with your estimate.

Can you replace just the garage or addition roof?

Yes. Tell us which roof area needs attention. We can discuss that section and how it meets any adjoining roof areas during the inspection.
